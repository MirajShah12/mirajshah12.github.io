## Project Overview

The **MLB Projections Engine** leverages historical performance data, Statcast metrics, and predictive machine learning models to forecast player performance for upcoming seasons.

### Key Features
- **Season Forecasting**: Predictive modeling generating wRC+ and WAR projections.
- **Overperformer & Underperformer Identification**: Statistical models isolating luck-driven variance from true-skill shifts.
- **Data Integration**: Automated ingestion of FanGraphs and Statcast metrics for data-driven player valuation.
